READ & PRACTICE Privacy Policy

Delete Your Read & Practice Account

You can permanently delete your account and its associated app data from inside Read & Practice.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Read & Practice app.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the notice and confirm deletion.

If you cannot access the app

Email support@adatepelabs.com from the address associated with your account and request account deletion.

Data deleted with your account

Deleting your account removes the associated account and profile, learning items, story progress, practice results, grammar and sentence data, and follower or connection records.

For more information about data handling, read the Privacy Policy.
